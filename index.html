Your privacy matters

Ayush_Tracker uses your webcam to detect if you're facing the screen. Here's what you should know:

  • Video is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere
  • Frames are analysed live in your browser, then thrown away
  • Only small numbers (timestamps, durations) are stored locally
  • Everything stays on your device — no accounts, no servers
  • Camera preview is off by default
